# 🛡️ Insurance Customer Behaviour Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **Insurance Customer Behaviour** to understand customer demographics, policy preferences, purchasing patterns, financial characteristics, and other key business trends.

The complete analysis was performed using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**, covering the full data analytics workflow from **data cleaning and preprocessing to visualization and business insights**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Understand insurance customer demographics and behaviour
* Identify important customer segments
* Analyze policy and insurance-related patterns
* Find trends and relationships within customer data
* Identify key business insights from customer behaviour
* Create interactive dashboards for business decision-making
